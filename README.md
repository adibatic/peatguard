# PeatGuard

A verification platform for tropical peatland restoration: hydrological
forecasting, fire-risk prediction, and satellite-based verification, built so
that both the communities restoring peatland and the funders paying for it can
see whether rewetting is actually working.

**Status: pre-code.** Still in research; the software described above hasn't
been started yet.

## Why

Fire risk on peat depends largely on one measurable thing: the water table.
That's the tractable part. The part that keeps restoration projects failing
isn't the hydrology — it's governance, financing, and trust: who owns the
data, who gets paid for a rewetted hectare, and how a village makes a claim a
carbon buyer will believe.
